How an order works

Four steps from “I’m looking for” to it being in your hands.

StepWhat happensYour part
1 Tell us what you want. A make and model, a caliber, or just what it needs to do. Send a request
2 We shop it. We check our distributor accounts for availability and price, then send you a straight quote. Approve it or pass

Email us about a transfer

  1. Email us before you check out. Tell us the seller and what you’re buying.

  2. We send the seller our license. They ship straight to us.

  3. We email you when it arrives. It’s logged in the moment it’s in.

Read before pickup

  • Bring a valid, government-issued photo ID with your current address. If you hold an Arizona concealed carry permit, bring it too.
  • Every firearm goes out through ATF Form 4473 and a federal background check. No exceptions, no shortcuts.
  • Federal law sets the age: 21 and over for handguns, 18 and over for rifles and shotguns from a licensed dealer.
  • Pickup is by appointment in Tucson. We’ll send the details when your order arrives.
